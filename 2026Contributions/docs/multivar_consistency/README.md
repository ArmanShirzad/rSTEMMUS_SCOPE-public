# Multi-variable consistency across engines

Data behind the 25-variable consistency check reported in the accompanying manuscript. No additional
simulations were run: every variable is taken from the archived outputs of the two-year Berlin-Steglitz
(DE-STG) runs, 2019 to 2020, 17,544 hourly timesteps.

Generated by [`2026Contributions/scripts/validation/multivar_consistency.R`](https://github.com/ArmanShirzad/rSTEMMUS_SCOPE-public/blob/main/2026Contributions/scripts/validation/multivar_consistency.R).

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `engine_consistency_by_variable.csv` | Correlation, RMSE and bias for each variable, for all three engine pairings: MCR against MATLAB, Octave against MATLAB, and Octave against MCR |
| `guard_ablation_by_variable_octave.csv` | The same 25 variables for the guard-ablation pair on Octave, guards reverted against guards applied |

## Variables covered

Net radiation, latent heat, sensible heat, canopy latent and sensible heat, assimilation, soil latent and
sensible heat, ground heat flux, transpiration, soil evaporation, total evapotranspiration, gross primary
production, net ecosystem exchange, canopy temperature, soil surface temperature, outgoing longwave
radiation, and soil temperature and soil moisture at 5, 20, 60 and 100 cm.

## Summary of the result

Across all 25 variables and all three engine pairings, agreement is at least r = 0.928. Net radiation and
canopy temperature are effectively identical, radiative and thermal quantities exceed 0.975, turbulent fluxes
and carbon terms lie between 0.945 and 0.996, and soil moisture is the weakest family at 0.928, as expected
of an integrated state variable accumulating small per-step differences over two years.

Applying the same breakdown to the guard ablation produces a depth-ordered pattern: radiation and canopy
temperature are unchanged, canopy fluxes fall only to 0.979, soil-side fluxes to 0.943, carbon terms to
between 0.919 and 0.938, and soil state degrades most and progressively with depth, from 0.832 at 5 cm to
0.599 at 100 cm. Nine of the ten guards act within the soil moisture and conductivity chain.

## Reproducing

```r
Rscript 2026Contributions/scripts/validation/multivar_consistency.R
```

The script expects the run directories named at the top of the file to be present under
`rSTEMMUS_SCOPE/output/`. Adjust those paths to point at your own runs.
