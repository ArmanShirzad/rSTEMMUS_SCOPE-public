Key conclusions for supervisor review
-------------------------------------
1. Engine parity on LE is excellent (MATLAB vs Octave r=%.3f, RMSE=%.1f W/m^2).
2. Octave (post-theta_r) matches MATLAB to within ~1.5 percent on r and 0.5 W/m^2 on RMSE.
3. The persistent +5 to +7 W/m^2 wet bias is present in MATLAB, MCR AND Octave -- it is a
   model-vs-EC artefact (likely surface-resistance parameterisation rss), not an engine bug.
4. No separate ET simulation was required: STEMMUS-SCOPE writes Sim_Theta.csv and
   fluxes.csv from the same coupled-physics loop. All metrics here come from the existing
   2yr runs that produced the SMC validation table.
